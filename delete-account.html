Delete Your Account

Optify · co.getsnippet.optify

You can permanently delete your Optify account and associated data at any time, using either method below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app

  • Open Optify
  • Go to Settings
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm

Your account and data are removed immediately and permanently.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can no longer access the app, email optify@getsnippet.co from the email address associated with your account and request account deletion. We will verify your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and account information (email, display name)
  • Photos you uploaded and the images they were edited into
  • Your edit history and saved results

What we keep

We retain a limited amount of anonymised billing and transaction records where required for legal, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes. These records are not linked to your identity and cannot be used to sign you back in.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are completed within 30 days of verification.

Questions?

Email optify@getsnippet.co and we'll help.
